## What this is

omgdiscount lists cloud, AI, developer and finance programs that founders can apply for, with the value of each and who it is for.

## Who maintains it

This list is maintained by the Datahashi team.

## How current it is

The figures and descriptions are sample data for review. Each provider sets its own terms, which change often. Check the provider's site before you apply.
