## What it is

The Fly.io Free plan is where every new account starts. Fly.io says it gives $1 of credit with no credit card, and $5 of usage every month once you add a card. The plan does not expire.

## What you can use it for

Running small apps on Machines, with volumes, data transfer, Sprites and one Managed Postgres cluster on the $4 Developer plan. With a card you get the full $5 in your first month and a new $5 on the 1st of every month. New Free organizations with a card also get a $30 Sprites credit, used before the $5.

## Who qualifies

Only your personal organization can be on the Free plan, and each card can be on only one organization's Free plan. Limits: 1 member, up to 3 Machines with no performance Machines, volumes up to 5GB each, up to 3 Sprites, one Managed Postgres cluster on the Developer plan, and community support. Extensions such as Tigris, Supabase and Upstash Redis, dedicated IPv4 and static egress IPs are not available.

If usage goes over the month's $5, the organization moves to Pay As You Go automatically and the whole month is billed at standard rates from the first cent. Check docs.fly.io/about/free-trial for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at fly.io/app/sign-up. You get a $1 credit that expires after 7 days.
2. Add a credit card to your personal organization to receive $5 of usage every month. Adding a card keeps you on the Free plan.
3. Watch usage on the Billing page so you stay under $5 for the month.
