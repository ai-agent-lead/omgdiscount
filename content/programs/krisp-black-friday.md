## What it is

Krisp's homepage and pricing page show a Black Friday banner: 55% off annual AI Meeting Assistant plans, with the code KRISPBF2026. Those pages do not publish an end date, a discounted dollar price, or whether the 55% off applies only to the first year.

## What you can use it for

Krisp's AI Meeting Assistant includes noise cancellation, meeting transcription, recording, and AI notes. Paid Meeting Assistant plans listed on the pricing page include Core and Advanced.

## Who qualifies

The official banner says the discount is for annual plans and gives the code KRISPBF2026. It does not say whether the offer is limited to new customers, which plans besides annual AI Meeting Assistant are included, or when the offer ends.

Check krisp.ai/pricing for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open krisp.ai/pricing and choose an annual AI Meeting Assistant plan.
2. Enter the official code KRISPBF2026 at checkout and confirm that the 55% discount is applied before you pay.
3. If the code is not accepted, check the banner on krisp.ai or krisp.ai/pricing, because the offer may have ended or the published terms may have changed.
