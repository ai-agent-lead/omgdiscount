## What it is

MongoDB Atlas offers a free-forever M0 cluster tier. Official pricing lists it at $0, for learning and exploring MongoDB in a managed cloud environment.

## What you can use it for

Hosting a small development or learning database. The free tier includes 512 MB of storage, 32 MB of sort memory and up to 100 operations per second, with shared RAM and vCPU. Outgoing data transfer is not charged on Free and Flex tiers. Backup services are not available on the Free tier.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can create an M0 cluster. MongoDB says it is a starting point for learning, with limited resources, and that you can scale up later if you need more.

Check mongodb.com/cloud/atlas/pricing for the current limits.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for MongoDB Atlas.
2. Create a cluster and choose the M0 Free tier.
3. Upgrade to Flex or a dedicated tier when you need more storage, performance or backups.
