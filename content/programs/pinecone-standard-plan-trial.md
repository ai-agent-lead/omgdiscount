## What it is

The Pinecone Standard trial lets you evaluate the Standard plan without up-front payment. You get $300 in credits over 21 days. The pricing page also describes this as a three-week trial with $300 in credits.

## What you can use it for

Standard plan features and limits, including bulk import, backup and restore, RBAC, all cloud regions, higher scale limits and Developer Support. If you started from the Builder plan, the $20 monthly Builder fee is not charged during the trial.

## Who qualifies

Each organization may take the trial once. Organizations already on Standard or Enterprise, or that first subscribed through a marketplace partner, cannot activate it.

When the trial ends or the credits are used up, quotas are blocked and console access is limited to billing until you add a payment method and stay on Standard, upgrade to Enterprise, or downgrade to Starter. You do not automatically return to your previous plan.

Check docs.pinecone.io/admin/billing/standard-trial for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Confirm your Pinecone organization is eligible for a Standard trial.
2. Start the free trial from pinecone.io/pricing or the console.
3. Use the $300 credits within 21 days. Before they run out, add a payment method, upgrade, or downgrade to Starter if you want to keep access.
