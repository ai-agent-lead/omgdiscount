## What it is

ElevenLabs' official Black Friday and Cyber Monday offer takes up to 55% off the first year of its Creator and Pro annual plans. The promo page says the offer runs from November 16 to December 1, 2026, and ends at 11:59pm ET on December 1.

## What you can use it for

A paid plan's credits can be used across ElevenCreative (image, video, music, voice, and sound effects), ElevenAPI, and ElevenAgents. After the first year, the plan renews at the regular annual price. You can cancel anytime.

## Who qualifies

The promo page says the offer is for new subscribers, anyone not already on Creator annual, Pro annual, or a higher plan, and Pro monthly subscribers who switch to annual. Creator annual is $99 for the first year, 55% off. Pro annual is 55% off the first year for new and upgrading subscribers, or 50% off when you switch from Pro monthly. The page does not publish a dollar price for the discounted Pro annual plan.

In the ElevenLabs app, new users get 50% off their first month, and Creator monthly subscribers can switch to annual at 55% off. The Pro discount is on the web only.

Check elevenlabs.io/black-friday-promo for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open elevenlabs.io/black-friday-promo or elevenlabs.io/pricing and choose the Creator or Pro annual plan, or follow the in-app offer if you are using the mobile app.
2. Subscribe before the offer ends at 11:59pm ET on December 1, 2026.
3. After the first year, check your renewal price on your ElevenLabs account if you want to stay on the plan.
