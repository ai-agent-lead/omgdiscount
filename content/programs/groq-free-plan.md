## What it is

GroqCloud has a Free plan and a paid Developer plan. Groq's rate limits page lists per-model limits for the Free plan, and its billing FAQ says upgrading to the Developer plan requires a payment method such as a credit card, US bank account or SEPA debit account.

## What you can use it for

Prototyping and testing apps against fast inference on open models. The Free plan table lists limits for models including gpt-oss-120b, gpt-oss-20b, qwen3.8-27b and whisper-large-v3, with higher limits on the Developer plan.

## Who qualifies

Anyone with a GroqCloud account. Limits apply per model and are summarized on the rate limits page; your exact limits are on the Limits page of your account settings. Groq's billing FAQ says it occasionally offers promotional credits, such as at hackathons and special events, but it publishes no standing startup credit amount.

Check console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits for the current limits.

## How to apply

1. Create a GroqCloud account at console.groq.com.
2. Generate an API key and call the models within the Free plan limits.
3. Upgrade to the Developer plan in billing settings if you need higher limits.
