## What it is

Grafana Cloud is a hosted observability platform for metrics, logs, traces, profiles and performance testing. Its pricing page describes the Free plan as always $0, perfect for personal projects, exploring new ideas and early-stage startups, with no charges ever.

## What you can use it for

Monitoring an app or small startup stack. The Free plan includes all Grafana Cloud services with usage limits, plus Adaptive Telemetry, the AI Assistant and community support, with 14 days of retention for metrics, logs, traces, profiles and k6 performance tests.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can use the Free plan. The pricing page lists limits of 10k active metric series per month, 50 GB ingested per month for each of logs, traces and profiles, and 3 active users each for Grafana and incident response. The metrics and logs tiers say no credit card is required. The Pro plan starts at $19 per month plus usage and adds longer retention and 8x5 email support. Check grafana.com/pricing before relying on the limits.

## How to apply

1. Open grafana.com/pricing and choose the Free plan.
2. Create a Grafana Cloud account and send telemetry within the free limits.
3. Move to Pro if you need more usage, longer retention or email support.
