## What it is

Railway is a hosting platform that bills by the second for what an app uses. New users can start a free trial.

## What you can use it for

Deploying small apps, services and databases. Railway's Free plan limits a service to 1 vCPU and 0.5 GB RAM and gives 0.5 GB of volume storage.

## Who qualifies

New users. Railway says the trial gives access to basic features for up to 30 days and includes a one-time $5 grant. After 30 days or once $5 is spent, the account reverts to the Free plan with $1 of free credit per month, which does not roll over.

Check docs.railway.com/pricing/free-trial for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at railway.com and start the free trial.
2. Optionally connect GitHub to be verified for the full trial.
