## What it is

Vercel AI Gateway's free tier includes a $5 monthly credit. Official pricing docs list this as "$5/month included." It is a recurring monthly credit, not a time-limited trial.

## What you can use it for

Calling a subset of models through AI Gateway at the provider's list price, with no platform markup. Free-tier requests have lower per-model rate limits. The credit starts when you make your first AI Gateway request.

## Who qualifies

Every Vercel team gets the free tier. Once you purchase AI Gateway credits, the team moves to the paid tier and the monthly free credit no longer applies. The free credit covers only free-tier models, not the full catalog.

Check vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway/pricing for the current amount and model list.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for Vercel or use an existing team.
2. Make an AI Gateway request to a free-tier model. The monthly credit starts with that first request.
3. Stay on the free tier if you want the $5 monthly credit to keep applying. Buying credits ends the free monthly allowance.
