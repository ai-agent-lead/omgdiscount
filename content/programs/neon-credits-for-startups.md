## What it is

Neon is a serverless Postgres database. The startup program on neon.com also offers Databricks credits on the venture-backed tier.

## What you can use it for

Production and development Postgres, including branching for CI and previews. Venture-backed startups can also use credits on Databricks.

## Who qualifies

Self-funded, early-stage startups with less than $1 million in funding can receive up to $1,000 in Neon credits, plus onboarding support and early access to new features.

VC-backed startups with at least $1 million in funding, or participants in a recognized accelerator such as Y Combinator, can receive up to $200,000 in combined Neon and Databricks credits, along with onboarding, early access and co-marketing opportunities. Both tracks are for teams building an early-stage product or MVP.

Credits expire after 12 months. When they run out or expire, the account stays on its selected plan and is billed normally for further usage.

Check neon.com/startups for the current amounts and terms.

## How to apply

1. Open neon.com/startups and fill out the application for the self-funded or venture-backed track.
2. Neon says it will get back to you within a few business days.
3. After approval, use the credits on your Neon account, and on Databricks if you received that tier, before they expire.
