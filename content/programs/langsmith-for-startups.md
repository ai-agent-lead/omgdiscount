## What it is

LangSmith is LangChain's platform for tracing, evaluating, deploying and monitoring AI agents. LangSmith for Startups is its program for early-stage companies.

## What you can use it for

Debugging and monitoring agents with tracing, running evals, and deploying agent workloads. The Scale tier adds credits against LangSmith usage, office hours and cohort sessions with the LangChain team, and event invitations.

## Who qualifies

There are two tiers, and both are for first-time LangSmith customers. Build is for startups with less than $10M in funding that have raised at least $25K in seed funding; it gives discounted seat pricing, and the page says additional eligibility criteria may apply. Scale is for Series A (up to $50M in funding) or earlier startups backed by one of LangChain's premier VC partners; it gives $10K in LangSmith credits for 1 year (2 years for Y Combinator companies).

LangChain does not publish the discount percentage for the Build tier. Check langchain.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Create a LangSmith account with your startup domain email.
2. Pick the Build or Scale tier on langchain.com/startups and apply with the form for that tier.
3. LangChain reviews the application and emails you with your benefits and activation steps.
