## What it is

Datadog for Startups gives early-stage companies up to a year of free access to the full Datadog monitoring and security platform, with up to $100k in credits.

## What you can use it for

Observability and security for your stack: all generally available Datadog products, plus new products that become generally available during your program. Participants can also sign up for Preview products if space permits, and can book optional sessions with a technical engagement manager.

## Who qualifies

Your company must be Series A or earlier and new to Datadog. You cannot be a current or past Datadog customer or have received Datadog for Startups credits before. All applicants must come through one of Datadog's official referral partners, which include VCs and other startup programs listed in the application form. Datadog does not accept direct applications.

Datadog says approval typically takes 3 to 5 business days. After the acceptance email you have 30 days to confirm credit activation or the acceptance expires. An account inactive for three months gets a warning and 30 days to start using products before it is removed from the program. Datadog's page does not state how the $100k is split by startup.

## How to apply

1. Ask your investor, accelerator or startup community contact whether they are a Datadog referral partner.
2. Apply on datadoghq.com/partner/datadog-for-startups and pick your referral partner in the form.
3. Set up a trial account while you wait, choosing the correct region because it cannot be changed later.
4. Reply to the acceptance email within 30 days to activate the credits.
