## What it is

Amplitude for Startups gives early-stage teams a free first year of Amplitude's Growth plan. It covers product analytics, session replay, experimentation and more.

## What you can use it for

Tracking how users behave in your product, watching sessions, and running experiments and feature flags. The plan is metered at either 100 million events per month or 200,000 monthly tracked users; these are alternative meters, not a fixed conversion.

## Who qualifies

Amplitude's standard offer is for startups with fewer than 20 employees and less than $10 million raised. Partner programs can have different requirements. Amplitude says YC companies have a separate deal.

After the free year, Amplitude sends a projected price 60 days before the year ends. You can move to the annual Plus plan at 40% off, or drop to the free plan of 2M events per month and keep your data and dashboards.

Check amplitude.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Create a free Amplitude account.
2. Complete the Startups application shown after signup. If it does not appear, go to Settings > Organization Settings > Plans & Billing and scroll to the application.
