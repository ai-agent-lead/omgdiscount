## What it is

Claude Campus is Anthropic's ambassador program for students who lead AI initiatives on campus. The official page states that the program is closed for Fall 2026.

## What you can use it for

There are three tracks. Undergraduates can lead a Claude Builder Club, with workshops, hackathons, demo nights and resources such as API credits for members. Master's students can lead Claude Campus Conversations. PhD students and postdocs can lead Claude Science workshops. Selected ambassadors receive a $3,600 cash stipend, subject to program terms. The official page does not publish a set API credit amount for members.

## Who qualifies

Students anywhere in the world can apply when applications are open, though the graduate and PhD tracks may start from a smaller pool of schools during the pilot. Applicants must be 18 or over and have work authorization in their country of study. Returning ambassadors have to reapply.

The official FAQ says applications open on 1 September and close on 12 September, reviewed on a rolling basis. This is a full school-year program, running from September 2026 to June 2027. The page now says it is closed for Fall 2026.

Check claude.com/programs/campus for the next application window. Do not treat the 2026 dates as a guarantee for a later year.

## How to apply

1. Open claude.com/programs/campus. As of 10 November 2026, the page says the program is closed for Fall 2026, so you cannot submit a new application.
2. Watch that page for a later window. The official FAQ still describes a 1–12 September application period for this cycle.
3. If your campus already has a Claude Builder Club, you may be able to join it as a member and use shared resources without applying as an ambassador.
