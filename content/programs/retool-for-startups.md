## What it is

Retool for Startups gives early-stage companies a 100% off coupon for one year on Retool, the platform for building internal tools and customer portals. Retool lists the value as up to $60,000.

## What you can use it for

Building custom internal tools and customer portals without paying for the plan in your first year. The coupon applies to the monthly Team or Business plan.

## Who qualifies

Startups that are bootstrapped, angel-funded, debt-funded, pre-seed, seed or Series A, have raised less than $10 million in total funding, and were founded within the last 10 years. You must be a new customer on the monthly Team or Business plan. Customers who have been on a paid plan for multiple billing cycles are not eligible. The offer cannot be used with other discounts from sales or moved to a different cloud account or self-hosted deployment.

Check retool.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Upgrade to your chosen monthly plan, Team or Business.
2. Submit the application on retool.com/startups.
3. After acceptance you get a confirmation email, and the discount applies to your first invoice at the end of the first billing cycle. After the year you get 25% off your paid plan the following year.
