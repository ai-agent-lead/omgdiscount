## What it is

The Vercel Open Source Program gives maintainers platform credits, a starter pack of third-party credits and community support. As of 10 November 2026, the official page says applications are currently closed.

## What you can use it for

If selected, a project receives $3,600 in Vercel platform credits over three years, to be used for the open source work itself. There is also an OSS Starter Pack with credits from third-party services, and priority community support from Vercel. After three years, projects graduate so new applicants can join. Vercel Marketplace providers are not covered by these credits.

## Who qualifies

Projects must be open source, actively developed and maintained, hosted or intended to be hosted on Vercel, show measurable impact or growth potential, follow a code of conduct, and use credits only for the project. New projects may apply. Fully open source nonprofits are welcome. Startups with open source projects may apply, but funded open-source companies are directed to the Startups Program instead.

The official page does not publish the next application date. Check vercel.com/open-source-program before you assume a window is open.

## How to apply

1. Open vercel.com/open-source-program. If the page still says applications are closed, you cannot submit a request.
2. When a window opens, apply from that page with a project that meets the criteria above.
3. If you are a funded open-source company, consider Vercel for Startups instead.
