## What it is

Claude for Nonprofits is Anthropic's discounted Team and Enterprise offering for mission-driven organizations, with nonprofit-specific skills, connectors and free training.

## What you can use it for

Running Claude and Claude Code for staff work such as fundraising, grant writing, program analysis and internal tools, at the nonprofit Team or Enterprise rates shown on the official page. Verified nonprofits also get the Claude for Nonprofits plugin, connectors including Blackbaud, Benevity and Candid, and free AI fluency courses.

## Who qualifies

Nonprofit pricing is open to registered 501(c)(3) organizations and their international equivalents, K-12 public and private schools, and certain mission-based healthcare providers, including independent Critical Access Hospitals, Rural Emergency Hospitals, Federally Qualified Health Centers and Rural Health Clinics.

On 10 November 2026, the official page listed the Team plan at $8 per user per month, or $3 per user per month for nonprofits based in low- or middle-income countries. Organizations with up to 150 seats can start on Team; larger organizations go through sales for Enterprise. A December 2025 announcement described discounts of up to 75%; the current page shows the per-seat prices above rather than a single percentage, so check claude.com/solutions/nonprofits before you rely on a discount figure.

## How to apply

1. Open claude.com/solutions/nonprofits and choose the Team plan if you need 2 to 150 seats.
2. Complete nonprofit verification with Goodstack. Anthropic says approval is usually quick, and you then get an email to finish setup with the discount applied.
3. Larger organizations, or those that need Enterprise, should contact Anthropic sales from the nonprofit page and complete verification during the sales process.
