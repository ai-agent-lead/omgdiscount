## What it is

Bordfeed's Black Friday page offers 50% off the first three months of a Pro, Max or Ultra plan with the code BFCM50. Pro is $39.50 a month instead of $79, Max is $149.50 instead of $299, and Ultra is $499.50 instead of $999. After three months the plan renews at the regular price. Yearly plans get 50% off the first year. The offer ends Friday, Dec. 4, 2026 at 23:59 CET.

## What you can use it for

Bordfeed is a job data API for agents and builders: 500,000+ live jobs from company hiring pages, refreshed every night, with closed roles removed. You get the jobs by REST API, webhooks, email digests or MCP for ChatGPT and Claude.

## Who qualifies

The offer is for new subscriptions only. Existing plans do not qualify.

New accounts start with $20 in free credit, with no card required.

## How to apply

1. Open bordfeed.com/black-friday and sign up with Google or email.
2. Pick Pro, Max or Ultra on that page or under Billing in the dashboard.
3. Enter the code BFCM50 at checkout and confirm the 50% discount is shown before you pay.
