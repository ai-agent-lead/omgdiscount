## What it is

The OpenAI Researcher Access Program offers subsidized API access for research on responsible AI deployment, risk mitigation and the societal impact of AI systems.

## What you can use it for

Up to $1,000 in OpenAI API credits, valid for 12 months, on any of OpenAI's publicly available models. Credits cannot be extended or transferred to a new institution, and they do not exempt you from OpenAI's usage policies.

## Who qualifies

Researchers with an active affiliation to an academic institution or other research organization, and nonprofits conducting research (not operational support), can apply. Applicants must be based in countries supported by the OpenAI API. OpenAI says it is especially interested in work from researchers with limited financial and institutional resources, and in topics such as alignment, fairness, societal impact, misuse, human-AI interaction and economic effects.

Applications are reviewed once every three months, in March, June, September and December. OpenAI says accepted applicants typically receive credits about 4 to 6 weeks after that quarter's review. Unused credits expire one year after they are granted and cannot be reinstated.

Check openai.com/form/researcher-access-program and the Researcher Access Program FAQ for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Read the program description, sharing and publication policy, and usage policies on openai.com/form/researcher-access-program.
2. Apply through the SurveyMonkey Apply portal linked from that page, with your research question, planned API use and affiliation.
3. Wait for the next quarterly review. If you are awarded credits, use them within 12 months on the organization named in the award.
