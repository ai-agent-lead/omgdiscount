## What it is

Mixpanel for Startups gives early-stage companies a free first year on the Startup Plan, a plan open only to program participants, for product analytics.

## What you can use it for

Tracking user behavior, running experiments, watching session replays and using feature flags. Mixpanel's docs list 1 billion events per year, 1M Session Replay recordings, Experiments with 1.2M MEUs per year, and Feature Flags with 50 flags and 120M API requests per year, plus add-ons such as Data Pipelines, Group Analytics and Warehouse Connectors. The $145,000 figure is Mixpanel's own stated value.

## Who qualifies

Mixpanel lists four requirements: incorporated less than five years ago, raised no more than $8M USD in total funding, not previously redeemed other Mixpanel offers, and never on a paid plan. You do not need a VC or accelerator referral. Startups can take part once.

You must begin sending data within 90 days of acceptance, or you are removed from the program and cannot rejoin. The plan does not support BAAs or MSAs. When the year ends, you move to another plan.

Check docs.mixpanel.com/docs/pricing/startup-program for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Confirm you meet the four eligibility requirements.
2. Apply through mixpanel.com/startups and pick your VC or accelerator in the form if you were referred, or choose no referral partner.
3. Your account is provisioned with the Startup Plan, then start sending data within 90 days.
