## What it is

Algolia is a hosted search and discovery platform. Its Startup Program gives eligible startups $10,000 in Algolia credits for the first 12 months.

## What you can use it for

Adding site or app search and discovery with Algolia's APIs. Under the program terms, the credits apply to Algolia services on the Grow or Grow Plus plan at list price. Algolia's page also lists credits for other SaaS products (such as Datadog and Notion), Algolia Academy resources and support.

## Who qualifies

Algolia's terms require that your company was incorporated no more than seven years before you apply, has raised less than $15,000,000 in equity funding, is not controlled by an entity that fails these requirements or competes with Algolia, and is not a previous or existing paid Algolia subscriber. If you have used another Algolia coupon code before, you are not eligible, according to the FAQ.

Credits are non-transferable, cannot be applied to a committed paid contract, and unused credits expire after 12 months with no extension. Algolia may stop selecting participants at any time.

## How to apply

1. Open algolia.com/industries/startups and choose Apply for Algolia credits.
2. Submit the application form with a valid email and company details.
3. If selected, Algolia emails you; the 12 months start from that notification.
