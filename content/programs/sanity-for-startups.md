## What it is

The Sanity Startup Program gives early-stage startups one year of free access to Sanity's Growth plan, a content platform for websites and apps. Sanity lists the value as up to $9,000.

## What you can use it for

Managing content for your site and apps with the Growth plan features, including 50 user seats, Private Datasets, User Roles, Comments & Tasks, Scheduled Publishing and AI Assist. Sanity says it only charges your card if you go above the included Growth plan quota.

## Who qualifies

Your startup must have fewer than 20 employees, have raised less than $5M in total funding, and not be a currently paying Sanity customer. Sanity says the program is currently available through portfolio companies of its partners, which include Sequoia Capital, Accel, Y Combinator, Creandum, Antler and Heavybit. After the year, the project moves to the Growth plan at $15 per seat per month, with a reminder beforehand.

Check sanity.io/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Check that your investor, accelerator or incubator is on Sanity's partner list.
2. Follow the link your partner gives you to join the program and create your first project.
3. Use the Growth plan for one year, and downgrade before it ends if you do not want the paid plan.
