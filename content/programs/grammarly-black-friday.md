## What it is

Grammarly's official Black Friday page offers 50% off Grammarly Pro. The discount applies only to the first term of the subscription. After that, the plan renews at the regular price unless you cancel first.

The page does not publish an end date. It describes the promotion as a limited-time offer that ends when the promotion ends. It also does not publish the discounted dollar price.

## What you can use it for

Grammarly Pro adds advanced AI writing support on top of the Free plan, including writing agents, context-aware assistance, and premium originality and citation tools.

## Who qualifies

The FAQ on grammarly.com/black-friday says the offer is available to new Grammarly Pro customers. If you are on the Free plan, you can upgrade to get the discount. Existing paid users can also use the offer, and it is applied to their next plan upon renewal. Discounts cannot be applied to previous purchases.

The discount applies to Grammarly Pro subscriptions. Enterprise plans are not part of the promotion. The offer cannot be combined with other discounts, coupons, or free trials. If you start a free trial, the discount will not apply once you are charged at the end of the trial. No promo code is required; the discount is applied automatically at checkout.

Check grammarly.com/black-friday for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open grammarly.com/black-friday and choose Claim offer.
2. Pick the Grammarly Pro subscription that fits your needs. The 50% discount is applied automatically at checkout.
3. Skip any free trial if you want this discount, because the official FAQ says the promotion cannot be combined with a free trial.
