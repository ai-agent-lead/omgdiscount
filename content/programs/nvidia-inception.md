## What it is

A free program for AI startups with NVIDIA GPU cloud credits, software and technical help.

## What you can use it for

Training and serving models on GPUs, and using NVIDIA's AI software stack.

## Who qualifies

NVIDIA's own page says the program is free, with no application or membership fees and no equity requirement.

Partner credit offers depend on eligibility and are not guaranteed.

## How to apply

1. Open nvidia.com/startups and start the Inception application.
2. Describe your company, your AI product and your website.
3. After acceptance, sign in to the member portal for software and support.
