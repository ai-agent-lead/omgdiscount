## What it is

A free program for AI startups with NVIDIA GPU cloud credits, software and technical help.

## What you can use it for

Training and serving models on GPUs, and using NVIDIA's AI software stack.

## Who qualifies

AI, data science and HPC startups building products on NVIDIA technology. Membership is free and is reviewed on the company's product and stage.

## How to apply

1. Open the Inception application and describe your company and the AI product you are building.
2. Submit the application with your website, team and stage details.
3. After acceptance, join the member portal to use the software kits and technical support.
4. Use the partner credit offers that Inception shares with members.
