## What it is

A free program for AI startups with NVIDIA GPU cloud credits, software and technical help.

## What you can use it for

Training and serving models on GPUs, and using NVIDIA's AI software stack.

## Who qualifies

NVIDIA says the program is open regardless of funding stage, with no application fees or equity requirement.

Some third-party guides list stricter criteria, such as an incorporated company under 10 years old with at least one developer and a working website. Check the current criteria on the application page.

Credit amounts depend on partners and eligibility; no fixed amount is guaranteed.

## How to apply

1. Open nvidia.com/startups and start the Inception application.
2. Describe your company, your AI product and your website.
3. After acceptance, sign in to the member portal for software kits and support.
4. Check the partner offers shared with members and keep your company details updated.
