## What it is

GitLab for Startups is a program for externally funded startups that are new to GitLab. It covers GitLab's single platform for source control, CI/CD and security.

## What you can use it for

Running your team's code, pipelines and security scanning on GitLab Ultimate, as SaaS or self-managed. Support is not included in the free year. Self-managed instances must enable usage and analytics features.

## Who qualifies

There are two tracks, both for new customers only and both capped at 20 users. Seed-stage startups (Pre-Seed or Seed with up to $5M in external funding) get a free Ultimate license for one year, then 50% off any tier in year two. Early-stage startups (Series A or Series B with up to $20M in external funding) get 50% off any tier in year one and 25% off in year two.

Self-funded startups cannot apply at this time. GitLab validates funding from a Crunchbase or PitchBook profile, or a VC or accelerator web page, and will not consider applications without it. GitLab says the program is offered at its sole discretion and may change.

## How to apply

1. Confirm you are new to GitLab and fit one of the funding tracks.
2. Submit the application form on about.gitlab.com/solutions/startups, with a link that shows your current funding status.
3. A GitLab team member will contact you to verify eligibility and help you get started.
