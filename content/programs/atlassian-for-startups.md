## What it is

Atlassian for Startups gives eligible startups the Premium editions of Atlassian Cloud products for $0 for one 12-month period.

## What you can use it for

Planning and tracking work in Jira, writing docs in Confluence, recording screen videos in Loom, and hosting code with CI/CD pipelines in Bitbucket. Atlassian lists Jira Premium, Confluence Premium, Loom Business + AI and Bitbucket Premium at up to 50 seats each. Jira Service Management and Customer Service Management are included at up to 10 seats, and Jira Product Discovery Premium at up to 10 creator seats with unlimited contributors. Atlassian Marketplace apps are not included.

## Who qualifies

Atlassian says your company must not be an existing Atlassian paid customer, must be VC funded or associated with a partner accelerator or incubator, and must not have raised more than US$10 million in external funding. Atlassian reviews every application and may ask for proof of funding. Each eligible startup can enroll for one 12-month period. Going past the seat limits means upgrading those apps to a paid plan. Usage-based pricing can apply in some situations, and the products are treated as Free or Beta Products under the Atlassian Customer Agreement.

Check atlassian.com/software/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open atlassian.com/software/startups and choose Apply.
2. Submit your company and funding details. Atlassian may email you for more information to verify eligibility.
3. If approved, use the products for 12 months. Before the period ends, you can renew or cancel each product.
