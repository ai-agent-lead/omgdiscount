## What it is

The ElevenLabs Impact Program provides free access to ElevenLabs technology for nonprofit organizations, people with accessibility needs, professors and their students.

## What you can use it for

Approved nonprofits get 12 months of free Pro access, which can be used for voiceovers, accessible audio, agents and other ElevenLabs tools in mission work. The page also describes a separate 1 Million Voices initiative for people with permanent voice loss.

## Who qualifies

Registered nonprofit organizations in good standing can apply for a nonprofit license. ElevenLabs says it prioritizes organizations working in healthcare, education and culture. Organizations that fail nonprofit verification, or whose activities violate the Prohibited Use Policy, are not eligible.

Verification is done with Goodstack. Approved nonprofits receive 12 months of free Pro access and can reapply when the period is ending. If you are already on a paid yearly plan and want to switch to the nonprofit offer, ElevenLabs asks you to contact support about a refund before applying the discount code.

Check elevenlabs.io/impact for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open elevenlabs.io/impact and start the nonprofit application.
2. Complete nonprofit verification with Goodstack.
3. After approval, use the 12 months of Pro access on your ElevenLabs account. Reapply before the period ends if you want to continue.
