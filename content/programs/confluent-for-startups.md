## What it is

Confluent for Startups gives early-stage companies free product credits to run Confluent Cloud, the fully managed Apache Kafka and Apache Flink service, for up to one year.

## What you can use it for

Building real-time data streaming and AI pipelines on managed Kafka and Flink. The program also includes two one-hour sessions with a data streaming expert, ongoing bi-weekly technical office hours, and access to a community of other program members.

## Who qualifies

Confluent describes the program as for early-stage startups and does not publish a credit amount or detailed eligibility rules on its program page. Confluent also runs an AI Accelerator, twice a year in 10-week batches of 10 to 15 selected early-stage AI startups, which adds mentorship on top of the standard benefits.

## How to apply

1. Open confluent.io/startups and choose Apply now.
2. Complete the application form.
3. Confluent reviews your application and tells you the credits you receive if accepted.
