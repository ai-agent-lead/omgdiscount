## What it is

Supabase offers a Free plan at $0 per month. It is a standing free tier, not a time-limited dollar trial.

## What you can use it for

Running Postgres with Auth, Storage, Realtime and Edge Functions included in each project. Official Free-plan limits include unlimited API requests, 50,000 monthly active users, a 500 MB database on a shared CPU with 500 MB of RAM, 5 GB egress, 5 GB cached egress, 1 GB of file storage and community support.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can start on the Free plan. You can have two active projects. Free projects are paused after one week of inactivity. Projects on paid plans are not paused.

Check supabase.com/pricing for the current limits.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at supabase.com.
2. Create a project on the Free plan.
3. Upgrade to Pro if you need projects that stay running, more disk, or the included compute credits on paid plans.
