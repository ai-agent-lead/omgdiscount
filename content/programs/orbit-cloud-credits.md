## What it is

A cloud compute provider for small and mid-size AI workloads.

## What you can use it for

Running experiments and small training jobs.

## Who qualifies

Small and mid-size teams running AI experiments.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for an Orbit account with a company email.
2. Submit the credits request with your project and expected compute.
3. Wait for the review; approved requests get a credit balance.
4. Track usage in the Orbit console.
