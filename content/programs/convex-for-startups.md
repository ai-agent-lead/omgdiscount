## What it is

Convex is a type-safe, reactive backend and database, with live updates and transactions, used to launch and scale apps without managing backend infrastructure.

## What you can use it for

Building or migrating an app backend on Convex Professional for up to a year, with no seat fees and a 30% discount on usage-based fees up to $30,000.

## Who qualifies

Convex says the program is for early-stage startups at Series A or earlier, with fewer than 20 employees, less than 3 years old, and less than $3 million raised. You need a valid company profile on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, PitchBook or another recognized deal flow platform, plus a live website or an MVP-stage product.

Before you apply, Convex asks that the team be on at least the Starter plan with a card on file. Y Combinator companies are directed to a separate Convex YC deal.

Check convex.dev/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Put your Convex team on the Starter plan or higher and add a card in billing settings.
2. Apply at convex.dev/startups/apply. Convex says every application is reviewed by a human on a rolling basis.
3. After a decision, use the Professional plan benefits and usage discount for the approved period.
