## What it is

MongoDB for Startups gives early-stage companies MongoDB Atlas credits and Voyage AI embedding and reranker tokens, with technical support. There are four tiers: Inspire for bootstrapped startups, Grow for early-funded startups, Innovate for rising VC-backed startups and Scale for rapidly scaling startups.

## What you can use it for

Running MongoDB Atlas, including vector search, and using Voyage AI models for retrieval. Atlas credits cover Voyage AI usage only when it goes through the Atlas API; otherwise Voyage AI tokens are provided separately. Credits apply to future Atlas charges only. Atlas Developer Support is free for the first 30 days of your subscription, and after that you can pay for it with your credits.

## Who qualifies

Your startup must be 7 years old or younger, Series B or earlier, building a single software product (agencies and dev shops are not eligible), and have an active company website and LinkedIn profile. Startups not previously accepted for the maximum credit offer can apply; past participants may get top-up credits case by case. New and existing Atlas users can apply.

Accepted startups receive up to $15,000 in Atlas credits, and the amount depends on the startup's stage and investment partner, such as a VC or accelerator. MongoDB's page does not publish the amount for each tier.

Activate your promo code within 30 days of receiving it or it expires. Credits last six months for $500 credits and 12 months for credits above $500, counted from activation. You can ask for an extension by emailing startups@mongodb.com before they expire, and MongoDB reviews each request case by case.

## How to apply

1. Apply through the form on mongodb.com/startups, choosing your funding stage.
2. MongoDB emails a decision, usually within five business days. Accepted startups get a promo code in the welcome email.
3. Redeem the promo code under Billing in account.mongodb.com within 30 days. You must be an Organization Owner or Billing Admin.
