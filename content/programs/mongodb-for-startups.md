## What it is

MongoDB for Startups gives early-stage companies Atlas credits and Voyage AI embedding and reranker tokens, with technical support. There are four tiers: bootstrapped, early-funded, rising VC-backed and rapidly scaling startups.

## What you can use it for

Running MongoDB Atlas, including vector search, and using Voyage AI models for retrieval. Credits can also pay for a Developer Support plan after its free first 30 days, which starts at $49 per month.

## Who qualifies

To qualify for the base tier, a startup must be less than 7 years old and Series A or earlier, focus on a single software product or service, have not been accepted into the program before, and have an active company website and LinkedIn profile. MongoDB's page does not publish the credit amount for each tier.

Credits last 12 months. You can ask for an extension, which MongoDB reviews case by case.

## How to apply

1. Ask your VC, accelerator or incubator to refer you. If they are not a partner, they can email startups@mongodb.com to apply.
2. If you have no VC or accelerator, apply through the form on mongodb.com/startups.
3. Redeem the promo code under Billing in account.mongodb.com. You must be an Organization Owner or Billing Admin.
