## What it is

Convex's Free and Starter pricing starts at $0, with built-in monthly resources on the Free plan. Usage beyond those limits is pay-as-you-go on Starter.

## What you can use it for

Personal projects and prototypes on Convex's reactive backend, including the database, file storage, search, crons, auth, Node.js actions and preview deployments, within the included amounts.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can start for free. Official included amounts on Free and Starter include 1 million function calls (then $2.20 per additional million), 20 GB-hours of action compute, 0.5 GB of database storage, 1 GB of file storage, 0.5 GB of search storage, 1 GB of database I/O, 3,000 search query-GBs and 1 GB of data egress, shared across regions. The Free plan also limits you to 40 deployments and 1,000 concurrent sessions.

Check convex.dev/pricing for the current included amounts.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at convex.dev and start on the Free plan.
2. Build within the included monthly resources.
3. Move to Starter pay-as-you-go, or to Professional, if you need higher limits or paid features.
