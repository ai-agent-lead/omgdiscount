## What it is

Auth0 is a login and identity service. The Auth0 for Startups program gives one year of everything in its B2B Professional plan.

## What you can use it for

Adding sign-in, organizations and secure access for your users and AI agents to a B2B product while you launch.

## Who qualifies

Auth0 lists these requirements: venture-backed with less than $5M in funding, less than $1M in annual recurring revenue, and less than two years since incorporation. The offer is not valid for existing paid Auth0/Okta customers (enterprise or paid self-service) or startups that were previously members of the program.

Check auth0.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open auth0.com/startups and choose Apply today.
2. After you are in the program, you have 12 months, then you move to one of Auth0's self-service or enterprise plans.
