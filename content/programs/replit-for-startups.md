## What it is

Replit is an AI platform for describing an app in natural language, then building, launching and growing it. Replit for Startups has two tiers, depending on who backs you.

## What you can use it for

Building and deploying apps, sites and tools on Replit. The Select Partner tier gives $25,000 in credits valid for 6 months, a dedicated onboarding call, a priority support community and co-marketing opportunities. The Partner Network tier gives one year of Replit Pro for new accounts, with $100 in monthly credits, access to the most powerful models, up to 15 collaborators and 50 viewers, private deployments, database restore up to 28 days and premium support. Existing Replit users on that tier get the equivalent amount in credits.

## Who qualifies

Startups backed by a fund or accelerator on Replit's Select Partners list qualify for the $25,000 in credits. Startups backed by any other recognized fund or accelerator in the broader Partner Network qualify for free Replit Pro, not the $25,000. Your fund decides which offer applies. Replit says to sign up with a company email, because applications from personal addresses such as Gmail or Outlook may not be approved. The page does not list the partner funds or a funding limit.

Check replit.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open replit.com/startups and fill in the short application with your company email.
2. Name the fund or accelerator that backs you, so Replit can place you in the right tier.
3. If approved, use the credits within 6 months, or the Pro plan for its one-year term.
