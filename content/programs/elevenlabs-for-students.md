## What it is

ElevenLabs for Students gives verified students free access to ElevenLabs voice and audio tools, including the Creator plan and ElevenReader Ultra.

## What you can use it for

The Creator plan covers ElevenCreative, ElevenAgents and the ElevenAPI for three months, which you can use for class projects, research, agents or other audio work. ElevenReader Ultra turns documents, PDFs, books and web pages into audio for a year.

## Who qualifies

ElevenLabs' students page offers the Creator plan free for three months after you verify student status. The company blog, last updated 3 November 2026, says this is for university students aged 18 or over in the US, Canada, the EU27, Australia and the UK, with more countries to follow.

Students and professors at verified institutions also receive one year of ElevenReader Ultra free when they sign up with a .edu or equivalent email address.

Check elevenlabs.io/students for the current terms and eligible countries.

## How to apply

1. Open elevenlabs.io/students and start the student offer.
2. Verify your student status to claim three months of the Creator plan.
3. Sign up for ElevenReader with a .edu or equivalent email if you want the one-year Ultra offer.
