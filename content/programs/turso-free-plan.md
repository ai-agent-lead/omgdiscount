## What it is

Turso's Free plan is a $0 tier of Turso Cloud. The pricing page says you can start free with no credit card required.

## What you can use it for

Prototypes and small applications on Turso databases. The official Free plan lists up to 100 databases, 5 GB of storage, 500 million monthly rows read, 10 million monthly rows written, 3 GB of monthly syncs, one day of point-in-time restore and community support.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can start on the Free plan. Paid plans (Developer at $4.99 per month, Scaler and Pro) raise the limits and add features such as longer restore windows. Limits can change, so check turso.tech/pricing before you rely on them.

## How to apply

1. Open turso.tech/pricing and choose Try Cloud free or Sign up on the Free plan.
2. Create a database within the Free limits.
3. Upgrade to Developer or higher if you outgrow the limits.
