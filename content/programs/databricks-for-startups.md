## What it is

The Databricks for Startups Program combines startup credits, hands-on guidance and access to the Databricks Data + AI Platform. Databricks announced the consolidated program on June 16, 2026, with credits that cover both Databricks and Neon.

## What you can use it for

Building and shipping data and AI products on Databricks and Neon. The program also offers Solution Accelerators (notebooks and best practices), partner and community access, and connections to Databricks's go-to-market teams.

## Who qualifies

The program page says qualifying startups can receive up to $200K in credits for Databricks and Neon, and that the exact amount depends on qualification. It does not list detailed eligibility criteria. Databricks's launch post says the program is built for early-stage, venture-backed startups, especially founders who recently raised institutional funding from pre-seed through Series A and are building on data and AI, and invites other founders to apply and be reviewed. Databricks works with a select group of VC firms, so backed startups may also be introduced through their investor.

Databricks also runs an invitation-only AI Accelerator Program, which is separate and cannot be applied to directly.

Check databricks.com/product/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open databricks.com/product/startups and apply directly, or ask your investor to refer you.
2. Wait for Databricks to review your application and confirm your credit amount.
