## What it is

Amazon Web Services: servers, serverless functions, storage and Bedrock for models.

## What you can use it for

Backend hosting, queues, file storage, and running model endpoints.

## Who qualifies

Early-stage startups. The Founders tier is for self-funded companies; the Portfolio tier is for companies backed by a VC fund or accelerator on AWS's partner list.

## How to apply

1. Choose the tier that matches your funding status.
2. Apply with your company details, website and a short description of the workload.
3. Wait for the approval email with your credit code.
4. Redeem the code in the AWS Billing console before it expires.
