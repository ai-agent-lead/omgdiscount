## What it is

Amazon Web Services: servers, serverless functions, storage and Bedrock for models.

## What you can use it for

Backend hosting, queues, file storage, and running model endpoints.

## Who qualifies

AWS says Activate is for pre-Series B startups founded in the last 10 years, with an AWS account on the Paid Tier plan, that are either new to Activate Credits or request more than they received before.

Founders tier is for bootstrapped or self-funded startups. You start with $1,000 in credits, and select participants may qualify for up to $5,000.

Portfolio tier is for startups backed by an Activate Provider, such as an accelerator, angel investor or VC firm, and needs that provider's Organization ID. The amount is up to $200,000, so $200,000 is a ceiling. AWS Credits for AI Startups ($200,000 or more) are invite-only, through your account manager, after Activate Portfolio.

## How to apply

1. Open aws.amazon.com/startups/credits, create an AWS Builder ID, and choose Founders or Portfolio.
2. For Portfolio, enter your provider's Organization ID.
3. Link your AWS account and submit. AWS says you hear back within 5 to 10 business days. After approval, redeem the credit code in the AWS Billing console before it expires.
