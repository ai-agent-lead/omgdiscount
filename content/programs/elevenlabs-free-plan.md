## What it is

ElevenLabs' Free plan is $0 per month and includes 10,000 credits each month, shared across its products.

## What you can use it for

Text to speech, speech to text, sound effects, voice design, music, productions, image tools and a limited number of Studio projects. Credits are charged per generation. Unused Free-plan credits do not roll over; rollover applies only to paid plans.

## Who qualifies

Anyone who creates an account is placed on the Free plan. ElevenLabs' pricing FAQ lists the Free plan as $0 with 10,000 credits. There is a cap on credits used in a single request, and some commercial features sit on paid plans.

Check elevenlabs.io/pricing for the current allowance.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at elevenlabs.io.
2. Your account starts on the Free plan with the monthly credit pool.
3. Upgrade if you need more credits or paid-plan features such as a commercial license or professional voice cloning.
