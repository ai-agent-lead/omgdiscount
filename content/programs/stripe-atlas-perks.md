## What it is

Stripe Atlas incorporates a US company (Delaware C corporation or LLC) from anywhere in the world and bundles perks for the new company.

## What you can use it for

Stripe says Atlas companies get $2,500 in Stripe product credits to use during the first year after incorporation, for no-code products such as Payment Links and Invoicing. Atlas also gives access to partner discounts on startup tools from providers such as AWS, Mercury and Carta. Stripe's pages quote different totals for partner offers by region, so check the Perks tab in your Atlas Dashboard for the current list.

## Who qualifies

Only companies incorporated through Stripe Atlas. Atlas has a one-time setup fee that Stripe's regional page lists as $500 and that includes government fees and the first year of registered agent service. Partner perks are claimed after your Atlas application is approved, and each partner can set its own eligibility rules.

Check stripe.com/atlas and docs.stripe.com/atlas for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Start an application at stripe.com/atlas and incorporate your company.
2. Once incorporated, activate Stripe Payments from the Atlas Dashboard to use the Stripe product credits.
3. Open the Perks tab in the Atlas Dashboard to claim partner offers.
