## What it is

The Vercel Hobby plan is a free tier for personal projects and small-scale applications. There is no billing cycle and no plan fee.

## What you can use it for

Deploying personal sites and apps within the included monthly usage, including 100 GB of Fast Data Transfer, 10 GB of Fast Origin Transfer, 1 million CDN requests, 4 CPU-hours, 360 GB-hours of provisioned memory, 1 million function invocations, and included Web Analytics and related events as listed on the Hobby plan page.

## Who qualifies

Hobby is for individual, non-commercial, personal use under Vercel's fair use guidelines. If you exceed a limit, you generally have to wait until 30 days have passed before you can use that feature again. Commercial work needs the Pro plan.

Check vercel.com/docs/plans/hobby for the current included usage.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at vercel.com. New personal accounts use the Hobby plan.
2. Deploy projects within the included limits.
3. Upgrade to Pro, or start a Pro trial, if you need commercial use, teams or higher limits.
