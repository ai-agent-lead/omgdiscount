## What it is

MongoDB for Educators gives verified teachers, faculty and researchers Atlas credits, curriculum resources and a free certification voucher.

## What you can use it for

The $500 in Atlas credits can be applied to an Atlas organization for teaching and research. Educators also get curriculum materials and, after completing a Developer or DBA learning path on MongoDB University, a free certification voucher.

## Who qualifies

You must be an educator, faculty member or researcher at an accredited high school, college, university or technical school, or an instructor at a bootcamp, with a verifiable school-issued email or documents that prove the affiliation. Students are not eligible for educator benefits and should use the Student Pack instead. Self-directed learners, early-stage startups, marketing departments and programs whose primary purpose is not educational do not qualify.

Atlas promo codes expire if they are not applied within 90 days. Once applied, you have 12 months to use the credits. Each code can be redeemed once, at the organization level.

Check mongodb.com/academia for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Apply at mongodb.com/academia with your name, institution, course and syllabus.
2. Educators at accredited institutions in the US, Spain or the UK are often verified immediately through SheerID. Other applicants, including bootcamp instructors, are reviewed by MongoDB, typically within 5 to 7 days.
3. Apply the Atlas promo code to one Atlas organization within 90 days. After you complete a qualifying learning path, MongoDB emails the certification voucher.
