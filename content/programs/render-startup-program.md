## What it is

Render is a cloud platform for launching and scaling web and AI apps, with persistent compute, workflows and dynamic scaling.

## What you can use it for

Hosting full-stack apps, services and AI or agent workloads on Render, with credits applied after approval.

## Who qualifies

All listed tiers require a new Render customer, Series A or earlier funding, and less than $50 million raised. Credits are valid for one year from approval.

The Prototype tier offers $500 and requires at least $25,000 in seed funding and less than four years in operation. Build offers $2,500, or $5,000 from select partners, if you apply through an enrolled partner. Scale offers $10,000 (under $1 million raised) or $25,000 ($1 million or more) with funding from an enrolled top-tier VC, and allows up to five years in operation. The AI tier offers $100,000 for AI-native, data-heavy or agent and LLM-driven products with at least $2.5 million in funding from an enrolled top-tier VC.

Check render.com/startups for the current tiers and terms.

## How to apply

1. Create a Render account. Render asks you to include your instance in the application.
2. Complete the application on render.com/startups, or apply through your accelerator or VC partner if you are seeking a higher tier.
3. After approval, Render applies the credits to your account. They are valid for one year from approval.
