## What it is

PostHog is a product analytics platform that also covers web analytics, session replay, feature flags, experiments, error tracking, surveys and AI observability. The startup program gives eligible companies credits to spend across the platform.

## What you can use it for

Credits can be mixed and matched across most PostHog products. From 14 September 2026, credits can no longer pay for AI tools such as PostHog Desktop, the PostHog Slack app, Replay Vision, PostHog AI and Inbox. Credits cannot be used to claim a BAA under the Boost plan, or to pay for onboarding calls and service packages.

## Who qualifies

The company must be less than 2 years old, have raised less than $5M, have signed up to PostHog on or after 1 January 2023, and use its company email domain in the PostHog account. Personal email addresses such as gmail.com are not accepted. Credits are issued once per founder and once per organization, at PostHog's discretion. Y Combinator companies have a separate deal and the two do not stack.

Check posthog.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Sign up to a paid plan in PostHog from an account that uses your company email address. You are only charged for usage.
2. Fill in the application form on posthog.com/startups.
3. PostHog applies the credit automatically if you are eligible and notifies you by email. The partner benefits come in a separate email after acceptance.
