## What it is

Project Galileo is Cloudflare's program of free cybersecurity protection for vulnerable public-interest websites, including humanitarian, civil-society, journalism and human-rights groups.

## What you can use it for

Participants receive Business plan benefits, including unmetered DDoS mitigation, DNS, SSL, WAF, CDN, Cloudflare Workers, Zero Trust application access, Gateway, and related security and performance features.

## Who qualifies

Cloudflare says organizations working in human rights, civil society, journalism or democracy can apply. Services are offered when a trusted partner brings or approves a recipient. You can also apply directly if you are not affiliated with a partner.

Applicants need a live website and should describe their mission, nonprofit status, public-interest work, and why the site is a vulnerable target. Cloudflare does not reveal participating sites without permission.

Check cloudflare.com/galileo for the current terms and partner list.

## How to apply

1. Contact one of the partner organizations listed on cloudflare.com/galileo and ask them to sponsor you, if you are affiliated with one.
2. If you are not affiliated with a partner, fill out the contact form on that page with the live domain, your mission, and why the site is at risk.
3. Once a partner approves you, or Cloudflare accepts a direct application, the Business-plan protections are extended to the site.
