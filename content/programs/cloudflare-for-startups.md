## What it is

Cloudflare's developer platform and global network, including compute, storage, delivery, and core security and networking features.

## What you can use it for

Credits cover usage-based services such as Workers, Durable Objects, D1, Queues, Vectorize, Pages, and R2 (R2 is capped at $10,000). Core security and networking features, including DDoS protection, WAF, CDN and DNS, are included at no cost.

## Who qualifies

Cloudflare says you qualify if you are a for-profit startup founded within the last 10 years, funded up to Series B, building a technology product, with a publicly accessible website, a public presence on LinkedIn, X or GitHub, a business email on your domain, and no prior approval into the program. Educational institutions, personal blogs, consultancies, agencies, MSPs and resellers are not a fit.

Tiers are $10,000 for bootstrapped or self-funded startups with under $1 million raised; $100,000 for startups with under $5 million raised and funding from an affiliated partner; and $350,000 for startups with $5 million or more raised from an affiliated partner. Credits last one year or until they are used up, whichever comes first, and the program is one-time per startup.

As of 1 December 2026, Workers AI is not covered by credits for incoming startups. AI Gateway is also not covered. Check cloudflare.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open cloudflare.com/startups and submit the application.
2. Cloudflare says review typically takes up to 48 hours, and approval is sent by email.
3. After approval, usage-based charges are deducted from your credit balance in the dashboard. Keep a payment method on file for any usage that is not covered.
