## What it is

Supabase Pro and Team plans include $10 per month in compute credits. Compute is billed separately from the plan subscription, and these credits offset that compute charge.

## What you can use it for

Covering the compute for one project on a Micro instance. Additional projects add their full compute cost. Supabase's example is a Pro organization with two Micro projects: $25 for the plan, plus $10 and $10 for compute, minus $10 in credits, for $35 per month.

## Who qualifies

The credits come with the Pro plan, which starts at $25 per month, and the Team plan. They are an included allowance on those paid plans, not a standalone grant.

Check supabase.com/pricing for the current credit amount and plan prices.

## How to apply

1. Upgrade your Supabase organization to Pro or Team.
2. The $10 monthly compute credit is applied automatically toward project compute.
3. Review billing if you run more than one project, because extra compute is charged in full.
