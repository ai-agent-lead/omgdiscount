## What it is

The Intercom Early Stage Program discounts the Intercom helpdesk and its Fin AI Agent for young startups.

## What you can use it for

Customer support and sales conversations in Intercom. The plan includes 6 Advanced Seats, 6 Copilot Seats, 20 Lite Seats and Proactive Support Plus (500 messages per month). Year 1 includes 300 Fin outcomes and 15 Fin qualifications per month, falling to 150 and 15 in year 2 and 75 and 15 in year 3.

## Who qualifies

Intercom lists three requirements: up to $10M in funding, fewer than 15 employees, and not currently an Intercom customer. The discount applies to usage-based costs for email, pro conversations over 1k per month, messages over 500 per month and seats beyond 6. It does not apply to extra outcomes or qualifications, or to phone, SMS and WhatsApp, which are charged at list price.

Year 2 and year 3 discounts are only for customers who stay on the program.

Check intercom.com/early-stage for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Confirm you meet the funding, team-size and new-customer requirements.
2. Apply through the Apply now button on intercom.com/early-stage.
3. Set up Intercom on the Early Stage plan once approved.
