## What it is

Kit's pricing page lists a monthly Black Friday offer: 50% off the first three months of the Creator plan. Creator is shown at $20 a month for those three months, then $39 a month. The footnote says the offer applies to the monthly Creator plan, for accounts with up to 500k subscribers, and that it ends Dec. 1. The page does not state a year for that end date.

The offer is not listed on the Pro plan. Annual billing is shown separately as a 17% saving, not as this Black Friday offer.

## What you can use it for

The Creator plan is Kit's mid-tier email and creator platform plan. It includes the Newsletter plan features plus visual automations, email sequences, the Kit MCP, A/B testing for subject lines, polls, SMS marketing, and removal of Kit branding, among other features listed on the pricing page.

## Who qualifies

The pricing page says the offer applies to the monthly Creator plan for accounts with up to 500k subscribers. It does not say whether existing paid customers can use it. Creator and Pro also have a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.

Check kit.com/pricing for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open kit.com/pricing and select the monthly billing toggle labelled as the Black Friday offer.
2. Start the Creator plan (or its 14-day free trial) and complete checkout at the $20-a-month promotional price before the offer ends on Dec. 1.
3. After the first three months, the plan is billed at $39 a month unless you change or cancel it.
