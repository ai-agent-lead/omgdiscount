## What it is

Linear is a product planning and issue tracking tool. Its startup program gives startups backed by a partner VC, accelerator or community a free period on the Business plan.

## What you can use it for

Planning, tracking and shipping product work with your team and your agents.

## Who qualifies

You must be affiliated with an official Linear partner, be a non-paying Linear user, and have fewer than 50 employees. You need to be an admin of your Linear workspace. Linear's page does not state how long the free period lasts. When it ends, the Business plan renews at the standard rate unless you switch plans or cancel first. Linear's free plan remains available to everyone.

Check linear.app/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Ask your partner for their dedicated Linear partner link, or enter your partner code when prompted.
2. Sign in to Linear as a workspace admin and follow the link to redeem the offer.
3. Before the free period ends, switch plans or cancel if you do not want the Business plan to renew.
