## What it is

Modal for Academics is a credit grant for academic researchers who want to run GPU and CPU experiments on Modal, without requesting quota in advance.

## What you can use it for

Training, evaluating and serving models, and other research workloads on Modal GPUs and CPUs. Modal's academics page also mentions research support from Modal engineers and PhDs.

## Who qualifies

Modal's pricing page says graduate students, labs and researchers can get up to $10,000 in free compute credits. The academics page does not itself restate a dollar amount, and the grant is decided on application, so the full $10,000 is a ceiling rather than a guaranteed award.

Check modal.com/pricing and modal.com/academics for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Review the academics program on modal.com/academics and the credit grant wording on modal.com/pricing.
2. Apply through the academics page.
3. If you are approved, use the compute credits on your Modal workspace according to the terms you are given.
