## What it is

Project Alexandria is Cloudflare's open source credits program. It offers recurring annual credits, with benefits that can vary by project.

## What you can use it for

Cloudflare lists upgrades to Pro, Business or Enterprise plans, higher Workers and Pages request limits, more R2 storage for builds and artifacts, and enhanced Zero Trust access, including Remote Browser Isolation. The exact benefits are set by Cloudflare.

## Who qualifies

You must maintain an open source project with a recognized OSS license, and operate solely on a non-profit basis in line with the project's mission. Acceptance and the size of the benefits are at Cloudflare's discretion. Credits cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash, and you need a Cloudflare account with a payment method on file.

Check cloudflare.com/lp/project-alexandria for the current terms. The official page does not publish a dollar amount.

## How to apply

1. Confirm your project has a recognized open source license and operates on a non-profit basis.
2. Submit a sponsorship request on cloudflare.com/lp/project-alexandria.
3. If accepted, use the credits on the Cloudflare account you registered, according to the benefits Cloudflare assigns.
