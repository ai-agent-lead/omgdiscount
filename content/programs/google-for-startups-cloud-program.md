## What it is

Google Cloud: compute, storage, databases and managed AI services.

## What you can use it for

Hosting model training and inference, data pipelines, APIs.

## Who qualifies

Google's page lists a Start tier ($2,000) for startups founded in the past 2 years with a working MVP, a clear business model and plans to seek venture funding soon. The funded Scale and Scale AI tiers range from $200,000 to $350,000 and are for startups with verifiable Pre-Seed to Series A funding from a known investor, within the last 3 to 5 years depending on tier. Non-venture funding such as loans, grants or friends and family does not qualify for the Scale tier.

For Scale, AI startups get up to $250,000 in year 1 and 20% up to an additional $100,000 in year 2. Other startups get up to $100,000 in year 1 and 20% up to an additional $100,000 in year 2. Third-party models are not covered. Program benefits also include 12 months of Google Workspace Business Plus (separate application) and monthly Google Maps Platform credits (separate application).

Google decides acceptance. Check cloud.google.com/startup for the current tiers before you apply.

## How to apply

1. Check the tier that matches your funding stage on cloud.google.com/startup.
2. Apply with a business email that matches your company website domain and your Google Cloud billing account ID. Google says most replies take 5 to 7 days.
3. After approval, the credits are issued to the Cloud billing account you chose in the application.
