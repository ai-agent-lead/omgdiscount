## What it is

Cloudflare for Students waives the upfront cost of the Workers Paid plan for eligible US students, so they can build on Cloudflare's developer platform for a year.

## What you can use it for

You get the Workers Paid allotments, including up to 500 Worker scripts, 10 million requests per month, 20 million included logs, longer CPU time per request, and 6,000 included build minutes. Usage above those allotments is billed at standard rates.

## Who qualifies

The current offer is for students in the United States who are at least 18 and have a verified .edu billing email. Acceptance is at Cloudflare's discretion. The $5 per month Workers Paid base fee is waived for up to 12 months. You still pay for usage above the included allotments, and you must keep a payment method on file.

After 12 months the account reverts to the Workers Free plan unless you upgrade. The offer cannot be redeemed more than once. Students outside the US can request that their school domain be added; Cloudflare says it is expanding the program.

Check cloudflare.com/students for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for a free Cloudflare account, or use an existing one.
2. In Billing, add a valid payment method and set your billing email to your .edu address.
3. Add the Workers Paid plan on the Workers Plans page. Cloudflare says the $5 monthly fee is waived automatically for 12 months, and you should receive a confirmation email.
