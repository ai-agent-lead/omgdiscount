## What it is

The Neon Agent Plan is a pricing and support plan for platforms, including app-generation agents, that create and manage Postgres databases on behalf of their users.

## What you can use it for

Provisioning and managing large numbers of Postgres databases through the Neon API, with Launch-rate compute, unlimited projects, a sponsored organization for your free-tier users, and up to $25,000 in initial credits on the paid organization.

## Who qualifies

It is for platforms that provision and manage Postgres for end users. Neon says the paid-tier credits are for platforms not already enrolled in the Startup Program. You must have an active Scale plan with a credit card on file, and Neon has to approve the application.

Once accepted, Neon moves the account to the Agent Plan and helps set up a sponsored free organization and a paid organization. The $25,000 in credits covers compute, storage and data transfer on the paid organization. Neon sponsors the free organization, so you are not charged for that free-tier infrastructure.

Check neon.com/programs/agents for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Put your Neon account on the Scale plan and add a credit card.
2. Apply on neon.com/programs/agents.
3. After approval, Neon switches the account to the Agent Plan and helps you set up the free and paid organizations. You then manage projects through the Neon API.
