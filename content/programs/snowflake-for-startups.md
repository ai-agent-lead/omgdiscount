## What it is

Snowflake for Startups is Snowflake's program for startups building on its AI Data Cloud. Its page lists a Startup Accelerator, a VC Community with free-usage programs through partner venture firms, a Startup Package and Snowflake Ventures.

## What you can use it for

Building data and AI applications on Snowflake. The Startup Package gives eligible startups free usage on their first contract. The page also promotes a Snowflake-run competition with a chance at up to $1 million in investment from Snowflake.

## Who qualifies

Snowflake says it is a great fit if data is a core part of your product, you use AI or machine learning, or you deal with large or complex datasets. The page does not publish a dollar amount, funding stage or other fixed criteria for the free usage. It says the Startup Accelerator partners with 20 global venture capital firms.

Check snowflake.com/en/why-snowflake/startup-program for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open the Snowflake for Startups page and choose the Startup Package or the Accelerator.
2. Or use the contact form linked in the page FAQ, and Snowflake's team will get in touch.
3. Confirm the free-usage amount with Snowflake before you plan around it.
