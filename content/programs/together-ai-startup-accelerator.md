## What it is

Together AI is a platform for model training, fine-tuning and inference. The Startup Accelerator is a selection-based program with compute credits and engineering support.

## What you can use it for

Serverless inference, dedicated endpoints, fine-tuning and instant clusters. Credits do not apply to Reserved GPU Clusters. Accepted startups also get forward-deployed engineering time, which scales with the tier.

## Who qualifies

Together AI describes the program as targeted and selection-based. Tiers are based on funding: up to $15,000 in credits and 3 hours of engineering time if you have raised up to $5 million; up to $30,000 and 6 hours if you have raised $5 million to $10 million; and up to $50,000 and 10 hours if you have raised over $10 million.

Check together.ai/startup-accelerator for the current tiers and terms.

## How to apply

1. Open together.ai/startup-accelerator and submit the application with details about your startup.
2. Wait for Together AI to review the application and respond.
3. If accepted, use the platform credits on eligible Together AI services. They do not cover Reserved GPU Clusters.
