## What it is

Postman for AI Startups gives eligible AI-native companies the Team plan for their whole team for three months, plus credits to try Postman's Agent Stack.

## What you can use it for

Working on the APIs behind your agents in one shared workspace. The 25,000 AI Credits cover Agent Stack products such as Fern, Astropods and Postman Passport, not seat fees. AI credits for core Postman are limited to 400 per user. Credits expire 3 months after activation, and extensions are possible at Postman's discretion.

## Who qualifies

Postman lists these requirements: equity funding from an institutional investor, a product that does not exist without AI (such as agents, LLM-powered apps or tooling for them), founded after 2020, headquartered in the US or Canada, and new to Postman as a paying customer. One grant per company.

Unless you cancel before the program ends, your account moves to the paid Team plan at list price for the seats in use. Postman says it will notify you at least 14 days before the program ends.

Check postman.com/postman-for-ai-native-startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Use the contact form on the program page and tell Postman about your startup and investors.
2. Postman verifies your funding and fit.
3. Sign the short agreement, then Postman sets up your workspace and credits.
