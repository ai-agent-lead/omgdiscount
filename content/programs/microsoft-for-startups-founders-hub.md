## What it is

Azure cloud with access to hosted AI models through Azure AI.

## What you can use it for

Hosting apps and APIs, calling hosted models without managing GPUs.

## Who qualifies

Microsoft says the program is for early-stage startups building software products, including AI-powered and cloud-based applications.

Microsoft says credits unlock in stages. After approval you can start with Startup credits for early development and testing, and unlock up to $5,000 through business verification. More credits unlock as your product evolves and you use more Azure services, up to $150,000 over time. Credits cover eligible Azure services such as virtual machines, App Service, Kubernetes Service, Azure OpenAI services and Azure Machine Learning. The page lists no funding requirement, and you can apply if you already use another cloud.

Check microsoft.com/startups for the current amount and terms.

## How to apply

1. Apply through microsoft.com/startups.
2. Complete the startup profile: company name, website, product and team size.
3. Wait for the decision, then activate the Azure credits.
