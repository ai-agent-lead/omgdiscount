## What it is

HubSpot for Startups is HubSpot's program for early-stage companies, covering its CRM for marketing, sales and service. HubSpot's page says it gives Seed and Series A founders 90% off Year 1, discounts on AI credits as you grow, and agents priced on results.

## What you can use it for

Running your pipeline, outreach and customer support on one system. The page also lists partner offers for members, such as 90% off Asana Starter or Advanced, up to 90% off Dropbox, DocSend and HelloSign, 12 months free of Gem and 1 year free of Make.

## Who qualifies

The page splits the program into a Seed track for companies under $2M raised and a Series A track for companies over $2M raised. It does not state further eligibility rules on that page, and it does not publish a dollar value for the discount.

Check hubspot.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open hubspot.com/startups.
2. Choose the Seed or Series A track that matches your funding and select Apply now.
3. If accepted, set up HubSpot under the program discount.
