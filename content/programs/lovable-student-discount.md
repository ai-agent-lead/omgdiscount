## What it is

Lovable is an AI app builder. Its student offer gives verified students a discount on a paid plan.

## What you can use it for

Building apps and sites on Lovable Pro, with 100 credits a month and every Pro feature, at half price. Lovable's example shows Pro dropping from $25 to $12.50 a month.

## Who qualifies

You log in with the email address your school gave you and confirm you are enrolled. Personal addresses such as Gmail cannot be verified, but you can request a review of your school's domain. Lovable says the discount is only valid for users on the free plan. It lasts up to 12 months, and if you cancel your subscription you lose the discount and cannot reactivate it.

Check lovable.dev/students for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Log in to Lovable with your school email.
2. Select Verify student status on lovable.dev/students and confirm you are enrolled.
3. Subscribe to Pro with the discount applied; no card is needed to verify.
