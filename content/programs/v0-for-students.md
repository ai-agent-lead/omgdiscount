## What it is

v0 is Vercel's AI app builder. The student program gives verified students at participating schools one year of complimentary access.

## What you can use it for

Building and iterating on UI and app prototypes in v0, using the monthly credit allowance, for a year after you verify.

## Who qualifies

v0's students page says you need to be currently enrolled at one of its active schools and provide a valid student email address. The FAQ on that page says students who verify their status at an active school receive $20 in credits per month for free for one year.

The active-school list is published on v0.app/students and can change. Check that page for the current schools and terms.

## How to apply

1. Confirm your school is on the active-school list at v0.app/students.
2. Sign in to v0 and verify your student status with your student email, from the billing settings link on that page.
3. After verification, use the $20 monthly credits during the one-year period.
