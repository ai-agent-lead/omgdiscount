## What it is

Temporal Cloud for Startups is a credit program for Temporal Cloud, the hosted durable execution platform that runs your workflows and retries failed steps for you.

## What you can use it for

Running your Workflows on Temporal Cloud. Temporal says $6,000 of credits could cover roughly 10 to 12 months of usage, depending on your use case. Once the credits are used up, Temporal bills you monthly for usage.

## Who qualifies

Temporal says startups must have raised funding, no more than $30M, and raised it within the last 3 years. You also need a Temporal Cloud account where you have run your first Workflow. Existing Temporal Cloud customers can apply. Credits are not transferable to another company.

Educators, faculty and researchers at accredited educational institutions can email incubator@temporal.io about credits and support.

Check temporal.io/startup for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Set up a Temporal Cloud account and run your first Workflow.
2. Apply through the Apply Now button on temporal.io/startup.
3. If approved, Temporal credits your account with $6,000 and contacts you to set up an onboarding call.
