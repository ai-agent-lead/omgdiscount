## What it is

ElevenLabs is a voice AI platform for conversational agents, text to speech, speech to text, voice cloning, sound effects and related audio tools.

## What you can use it for

Building voice agents, speech features or other audio products on the ElevenLabs platform for 12 months, with a grant of 33 million characters (over 680 hours of Agents usage).

## Who qualifies

ElevenLabs says the program is for startups or companies with fewer than 25 employees when they apply, a business or monetization strategy, and a valid business email. Grants go to companies building a long-term product, not one-off projects or campaigns, and not products for children 18 or under.

Agencies, consulting firms and SMBs are not eligible. One application per company. Paying self-serve customers can apply; existing enterprise customers cannot. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, with no deadline.

After 12 months, or once 10,000 credits remain, or if the 33 million characters are used up earlier, the account converts to the Free plan unless you choose otherwise. Grants cannot be resold or exchanged for cash.

Check elevenlabs.io/startup-grants for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Fill out the application on elevenlabs.io/startup-grants with details about your startup, team and growth plans.
2. ElevenLabs says it will let you know if you are accepted within one week.
3. If the grant is awarded, ElevenLabs applies the grant plan and credits to the account you applied with.
