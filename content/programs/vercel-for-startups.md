## What it is

A hosting platform for web apps and frontends, with serverless functions.

## What you can use it for

Deploying a Next.js or web frontend with preview links for every change.

## Who qualifies

Early-stage startups with a web product or frontend. Eligibility is checked against company stage and team, and the program is subject to Vercel's current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open the Vercel for Startups application form.
2. Enter your company details and a link to the product.
3. Wait for review; approved teams receive a credit code.
4. Apply the code to your Vercel team under billing.
