## What it is

ElevenLabs publishes first-month promotional prices on some monthly plans, and annual billing that works out to two months free.

## What you can use it for

Paying less for the first month of Starter or Creator, or switching a paid plan to annual billing so you pay for 10 months rather than 12.

## Who qualifies

These prices are listed on elevenlabs.io/pricing. As of 10 November 2026, Starter is $1 for the first month, then $6 per month, and Creator is $11 for the first month, then $22 per month. The FAQ says annual billing equals two months free (annual price = monthly price × 10), with equivalent monthly prices of $5 for Starter, $18.33 for Creator, $82.50 for Pro, $249.17 for Scale and $825 for Business.

Check the pricing page before you subscribe; first-month offers can change.

## How to apply

1. Open elevenlabs.io/pricing and choose Starter, Creator or another paid plan.
2. For the first-month price, start a monthly subscription on Starter or Creator.
3. For the two-months-free rate, choose annual billing on the plan you want.
