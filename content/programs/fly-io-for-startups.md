## What it is

Fly.io runs hardware-isolated microVMs from Docker images, with Managed Postgres, volumes and private networking.

## What you can use it for

Hosting full-stack apps and backends close to users. Credits apply to usage on Machines, Sprites, Managed Postgres, volumes and bandwidth.

## Who qualifies

Fly.io's startup page says the program is for Seed or Series A startups backed by a venture firm or an accelerator that have not received Fly.io startup credits before.

Bootstrapped or self-funded companies are not eligible. Fly.io may ask you to verify funding with an email intro to your investor or accelerator. Credits expire one year after they are added, and they do not cover support plans, compliance packages or Machine reservations.

Check fly.io/startups for the current terms before you apply.

## How to apply

1. Fill out the application on fly.io/startups. You do not need a Fly.io account to apply, but credits land in a Fly.io organization, so create one and put its name in the form if you can.
2. A human reviews the application and replies by email within a week.
3. After approval, the credits are added to your Fly.io organization. Fly.io also invites you to a shared Slack channel.
