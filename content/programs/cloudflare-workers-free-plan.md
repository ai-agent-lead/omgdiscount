## What it is

By default, Cloudflare accounts use the Workers Free plan. There is no monthly Workers subscription fee. Usage is capped with daily limits rather than a dollar credit.

## What you can use it for

Building and running Workers and Pages Functions within the Free plan limits, including 100,000 Worker requests per day, 10 milliseconds of CPU time per invocation, limited Workers KV and Hyperdrive usage, and included daily or monthly amounts for Queues, D1, Durable Objects, R2 and related products as listed on the pricing page. Daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC.

## Who qualifies

The Free plan is the default for Workers users. If you exceed a daily limit, further operations of that type fail until the limit resets. The Workers Paid plan is a separate $5 per month subscription with higher included usage. The Workers plan is separate from any Cloudflare website plan you may already have.

Check developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing for the current limits.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for a Cloudflare account, or use an existing one.
2. Create a Worker. You start on the Free plan automatically.
3. Stay within the daily Free limits, or upgrade to Workers Paid in the dashboard if you need the higher monthly allotments.
