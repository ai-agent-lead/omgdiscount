## What it is

Claude Max, Ultra and Team plans include monthly credits for the Claude API. You claim them by linking a Claude Console organization to your plan. You do not need a payment method on Claude Platform to use these credits.

## What you can use it for

The Claude API, Claude Managed Agents, the Claude Agent SDK and the Console playground. Credits cannot be used for Claude Code, extra usage in the Claude apps, or Claude on AWS, Bedrock, Google Cloud or Microsoft Foundry. Unused credits expire at the end of each billing cycle, or monthly on annual plans, and do not roll over.

## Who qualifies

Eligible plans are Max, Ultra and Team, including discounted Team plans. Free, Pro and Enterprise are not eligible. New subscribers can claim after seven days. Official amounts are $100 per month for Max, $200 for Ultra, $20 per Team Standard seat and $100 per Team Premium seat, pooled and capped at $500 per month on Team.

Check platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/api-credits-for-subscribers for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Wait until your Max, Ultra or Team plan has been active for seven days.
2. On claude.ai, open Billing settings, choose the Console organization that should receive the credits, and accept the terms.
3. Use the promotional credits in that organization during the billing cycle. Purchase extra Console credits if you need to keep going after they run out.
