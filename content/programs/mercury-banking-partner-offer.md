## What it is

Business banking with accounts, cards and transfers for startups.

## What you can use it for

Operating account, paying vendors and contractors, and receiving payments.

## Who qualifies

US startups with a registered business entity, an EIN and a beneficial owner. Mercury approves accounts on its own review, so the offer does not guarantee approval.

## How to apply

1. Start the Mercury application and enter your company and owner details.
2. Upload the formation documents and EIN confirmation when asked.
3. Wait for Mercury's review; this can take a few days.
4. After approval, open the account from the partner link to receive the offer.
