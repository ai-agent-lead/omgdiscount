## What it is

Business banking with accounts, cards and transfers for startups.

## What you can use it for

Operating account, paying vendors and contractors, and receiving payments.

## Who qualifies

Mercury requires a US business entity and a passed compliance review. Sole proprietors are not eligible.

Partner perks are described differently across aggregator sites, and some sources say the older Mercury Raise program has closed. The perk value here is not verified.

Perk claims from other channels can reduce or block a new claim.

## How to apply

1. Open mercury.com and start the application with your entity details.
2. Upload the formation documents and EIN confirmation when asked.
3. Wait for Mercury's compliance review.
4. Check the current perks with Mercury before you rely on them.
