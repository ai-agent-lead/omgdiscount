## What it is

Neon's Free plan is a permanent $0 plan, not a time-limited trial and not a dollar credit. Neon says it does not require a credit card.

## What you can use it for

Prototypes, side projects and small apps on serverless Postgres. Each project includes 100 CU-hours of compute per month, 1 GB of Postgres storage (20 GB across the account), 5 GB of public network transfer, 10 branches and scale-to-zero when idle. Compute is only used while the database is active.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can sign up. If a project uses up its monthly CU-hours or transfer, compute is suspended until the next billing period. Storage over the limit blocks operations that increase size; data is not deleted. Neon also offers Claimable Neon, an instant project with no account that you must claim within 72 hours to keep.

Check neon.com/pricing for the current allowances.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at neon.com. No credit card is required for the Free plan.
2. Create a project and use the included monthly allowances.
3. Upgrade to Launch or Scale if you need more than the Free plan limits, or ask Neon about credits if you want to stay on Free with more room.
