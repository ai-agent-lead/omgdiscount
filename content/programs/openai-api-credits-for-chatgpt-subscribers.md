## What it is

Eligible ChatGPT Pro and Business subscriptions can claim free OpenAI API credits as a subscription benefit, by linking the plan to an API organization you own.

## What you can use it for

Paid API Platform usage on the linked organization, including the Responses, Agents, Decisions, Images and Realtime APIs and related tools. Credits do not cover Codex usage through API keys, and they do not raise ChatGPT or signed-in Codex limits. Unused credits expire at the end of the billing cycle and do not roll over.

## Who qualifies

You need an active, paid ChatGPT Pro or Business subscription that is at least seven days old. Free trials and complimentary subscriptions do not qualify. You must be an owner of both the ChatGPT subscription or Business workspace and the API organization, using the same email address.

Official monthly amounts are $100, $200 or $500 for the corresponding Pro tiers, $20 per paid Business Standard seat, and $100 per paid Business Premium seat. Business credits are pooled and capped at $500 per workspace per month. Annual Business workspaces receive the year's grant in one issuance, which expires at the end of the annual cycle. The first grant is prorated.

Check developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/chatgpt-api-credits for the current terms. OpenAI does not currently publish a general signup trial credit for new API accounts.

## How to apply

1. Confirm your ChatGPT Pro or Business plan has been active for at least seven days.
2. Start the claim flow from ChatGPT billing settings, or from API Platform billing, and link the subscription to an API organization you own.
3. Use the credits on that organization before the billing cycle ends. Add paid API credits if you want to keep going after the grant runs out.
