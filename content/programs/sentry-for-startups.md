## What it is

Sentry for Startups is a credit program for the Sentry application monitoring and error tracking service. Approved startups get credits and priority support.

## What you can use it for

Paying for Sentry usage on your organization, such as error monitoring, tracing and session replay, with the credits applied for 12 months.

## Who qualifies

Sentry lists three requirements: the company was founded in the last 2 years, it has raised less than $5M in venture capital, and it is new to paying for Sentry. You need a free Sentry account first, because the form asks for your organization slug. Founders in YC or a16z speedrun have a separate deal that does not stack with this one.

Check sentry.io/for/startups/apply for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Create a free Sentry account.
2. Fill in the form at sentry.io/for/startups/apply with your startup name and website, organization slug, founders, contact email, founding date and funding details.
