## What it is

Figma for Education is a free plan for verified students and educators. Figma says Figma and FigJam are 100% free for students, educators and schools, with no freemium or hidden upgrades.

## What you can use it for

Designing and prototyping in Figma Design, brainstorming in FigJam, and building apps with Figma Make and the AI tools on the canvas. Figma also lists Figma Draw, Figma Motion, Figma Slides and Dev Mode with its MCP server among the features.

## Who qualifies

To use the self-serve plan you must be a student or educator over 13 in a structured classroom, which Figma defines as a virtual or in-person education program. Examples are high schools, universities, trade schools, online courses, bootcamps, workshops and coding academies. You sign up at figma.com/education/apply, verify your education status and create an Education team. Higher education and bootcamps get the Professional tier. Using Figma with students under 13 needs the school to sign Figma's terms for schools, which gives the Enterprise tier to K-12 districts. In Japan, Figma says it may only be used by students above the age of consent unless the school signs those terms.

Check figma.com/education for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Go to figma.com/education/apply and sign in or create a Figma account.
2. Verify your education status and create an Education team.
3. Start designing on the free Professional tier.
