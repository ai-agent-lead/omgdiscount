## What it is

Pinecone's Starter plan is free, and is described as a way to try the product and run small applications.

## What you can use it for

On-demand indexes, inference and Assistant with Starter limits. Official limits include up to 2 GB of storage, 2 million write units and 1 million read units per month, 1 GB of egress, one project, up to two users, five indexes in AWS us-east-1, and included monthly inference tokens as listed on the pricing page.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can start for free. Starter is limited to the AWS us-east-1 region and does not include features such as backups, bulk import, RBAC or all reranking models. Pinecone also points small personal projects to Starter or the $20 per month Builder plan, rather than the Standard trial.

Check pinecone.io/pricing for the current limits.

## How to apply

1. Sign up at pinecone.io and start on the Starter plan.
2. Create an index within the Starter limits.
3. Upgrade to Builder or start a Standard trial if you need more regions, limits or production features.
