## What it is

JetBrains supports active open source projects with complimentary licenses for its IDEs and tools. It says it backs more than 6,000 projects.

## What you can use it for

Developing your non-commercial open source project. The licenses cannot be used for paid services, work projects or other commercial activity.

## Who qualifies

JetBrains says a project should have documentation such as a README, code of conduct or contributing guide, be a piece of software rather than samples or blogs, have commits every month before applying (checked over the past six months), and use an open source license with public source code. Licenses are for core committers whose contributions are regular and visible. The project must offer only free software with no associated commercial services, be maintained voluntarily by its developers, and not be continuously funded by commercial companies or organizations. JetBrains can grant or deny access or change the requirements at any time.

Licenses are valid for one year and can be renewed if the project still meets the requirements.

Check jetbrains.com/community/opensource for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. If you are a project lead or core contributor, fill out the request form at jetbrains.com/shop/eform/opensource.
2. Provide your project details and a copy of your software's license terms. Include any previous JetBrains open source license ID.
