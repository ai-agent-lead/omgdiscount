## What it is

Clerk provides authentication and user management. Every account gets 50,000 monthly retained users and 100 monthly retained organizations free. Clerk Pro adds SMS codes, allowlists and blocklists, RBAC, custom session duration, and device tracking and revocation.

## What you can use it for

Adding sign-in and user management to a product, then upgrading to Pro features at a discount once you need them.

## Who qualifies

Clerk says your team is eligible up to 1 year after launch with up to $5 million in venture funding. Clerk's page does not publish the size or length of the discount.

Your discount period starts as soon as your application is approved, so apply when you are ready to upgrade.

## How to apply

1. Sign in to a Clerk account that has an application, or create an application first.
2. Complete the form on clerk.com/startups.
3. Wait for approval. The discount period starts on approval.
