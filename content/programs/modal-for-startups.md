## What it is

Modal is a code-first cloud for running and autoscaling GPU and CPU workloads, including model training, inference and data jobs.

## What you can use it for

Training, serving and iterating on AI workloads without managing clusters. The startup program also includes technical support from Modal's engineering team and go-to-market help on launches and raises.

## Who qualifies

Applicants must be new to Modal. Credits are a one-time grant, and you may not reapply once you have received credits in a given tier.

The Seed to Series A track is for startups that raised any amount of VC funding from Modal's VC partner network, or more than $1 million in VC funding from any fund. A Scaling and Series B+ track is for companies that raised more than $30 million or are post-Series B, with a VC in Modal's partner network.

Modal's page describes the grant as "thousands of free GPU credits" but does not publish a dollar amount. Check modal.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Confirm you are a new Modal customer and that your funding matches a listed track.
2. Apply on modal.com/startups.
3. After approval, use the one-time credit grant on your Modal workspace according to the terms you are given.
