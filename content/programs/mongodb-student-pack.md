## What it is

The MongoDB Student Pack, offered through the GitHub Student Developer Pack, gives eligible students Atlas credits and a free MongoDB certification.

## What you can use it for

The $50 in Atlas credits can be applied to a MongoDB Atlas organization for database hosting. After you complete a certification learning path on MongoDB University, you can take the certification exam at no charge. MongoDB values the certification at $150 and describes the pack as $200 of benefits.

## Who qualifies

MongoDB says the benefits are available through the GitHub Student Developer Pack. To qualify for that pack, you must be enrolled in a degree- or diploma-granting program, have a verifiable school-issued email or documents confirming student status, have a GitHub personal account, and be at least 13 years old.

To get the free certification, you also need to verify student status with your GitHub information at mongodb.com/students and complete the learning path for that certification. Atlas promo codes are redeemed once, at the organization level, and applying them requires a card or PayPal on the Atlas account.

Check mongodb.com/students and the GitHub Student Developer Pack for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Join the GitHub Student Developer Pack and meet GitHub's student eligibility rules.
2. Open mongodb.com/students, verify your student status with your GitHub account, and claim the Atlas credit perk.
3. Apply the Atlas promo code to one Atlas organization. Complete a MongoDB University certification path to receive the free exam voucher.
