## What it is

Upstash Redis is a serverless Redis database with a REST API. Its pricing page lists a Free plan at $0 per month.

## What you can use it for

Caching, sessions, queues and small apps on a managed Redis database. The Free plan lists 250 MB of max data, 10 GB of max bandwidth and 10,000 commands per second, with persistence and the REST API. The FAQ says the free tier includes 250 MB of data and 500K commands per month.

## Who qualifies

Anyone can sign up. A free account can have one database. The $200 per month Prod Pack, which brings an uptime SLA and multi-zone high availability, is not available on the Free tier. Paid options are Pay as You Go at $0.20 per 100K commands, and Fixed plans from $10 per month. Check upstash.com/pricing/redis for current limits.

## How to apply

1. Open upstash.com/pricing/redis and sign up for the Free plan.
2. Create one Redis database on AWS, GCP or Vercel.
3. Upgrade to Pay as You Go or a Fixed plan if you pass the free limits.
