## What it is

A workspace for docs, wikis, databases and project tracking.

## What you can use it for

Team docs, roadmaps, and light CRM or hiring trackers.

## Who qualifies

You must be a non-paying Notion customer with a work email (not Gmail, Outlook and similar), have fewer than 100 employees and have received no previous Notion promotions. The length depends on who you are. Startups affiliated with one of Notion's select startup partners get 6 months free. Others with a valid business website and company domain email get 3 months. Companies with fewer than 10 employees, service-based businesses, or applicants Notion cannot fully verify get 1 month.

Notion says the Business plan costs $20 per member paid annually, and the offer is worth up to $12,000 for 100 employees. Notion Certified Consultants also offer one complimentary setup session.

Check notion.com/startups for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open notion.com/startups and fill in the application with your company details, including your startup partner if you have one.
2. Notion decides the length of the offer from your application and emails the result.
3. Redeem the offer on your workspace when you upgrade to the Business plan.
