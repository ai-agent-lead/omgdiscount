## What it is

A workspace for docs, wikis, databases and project tracking.

## What you can use it for

Team docs, roadmaps, and light CRM or hiring trackers.

## Who qualifies

The offer needs a partner's code. Only net new customers qualify, and each offer can be redeemed once.

Check notion.com/startups for the current length and plan.

## How to apply

1. Get the code from your partner, or start from notion.com/startups.
2. Fill in the startup form and company details.
3. Apply the offer when you upgrade your workspace plan.
