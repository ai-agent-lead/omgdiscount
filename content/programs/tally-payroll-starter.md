## What it is

Payroll and benefits software for small teams.

## What you can use it for

Running payroll for your first hires.

## Who qualifies

Companies with a few employees that are running payroll for the first time.

## How to apply

1. Create a Tally account and enter your company details.
2. Add your first employees and their pay details.
3. Apply the starter discount at the first payroll run.
4. Confirm tax filings are set up before the first pay date.
