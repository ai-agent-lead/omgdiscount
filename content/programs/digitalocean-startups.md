## What it is

DigitalOcean Startups, formerly called Hatch, gives startups 12 months of DigitalOcean credits through approved accelerator, VC and incubator partners. As of 10 November 2026, DigitalOcean says applications for the current cohort are paused while it prepares an updated program, scheduled for early 2027.

## What you can use it for

Core credits cover DigitalOcean services except GPU Droplets, NVIDIA H100 GPU Kubernetes, Bare Metal GPUs, Dedicated Inference, third-party hosted AI models, Paperspace and Cloudways. GPU credits are a separate benefit, offered to select startups after review. Participants generally have a $10,000 monthly limit on credit use.

## Who qualifies

DigitalOcean prioritizes AI-native startups that have raised $10M or less and have a company website and matching company email. Service-based startups such as agencies are not eligible. The credit amount comes through your partner, so DigitalOcean does not publish one figure. Active members keep their credits and perks until their original term ends.

Check digitalocean.com/hatch for the current status.

## How to apply

1. Open digitalocean.com/hatch. While applications are paused, you cannot submit a new request.
2. Ask your accelerator, VC or incubator whether it is a DigitalOcean Startups partner, and check again when the new program launches in early 2027.
3. Create a DigitalOcean team account with a company email and a valid card before you apply.
