## What it is

Kiro is AWS's agentic AI IDE. The Kiro Startup Credits program gives eligible startups up to a year of Kiro Pro+ credits, deposited into their AWS account.

## What you can use it for

Using Kiro Pro+ for spec-driven AI coding across your team. The tier you pick sets the number of users: Starter covers up to 2, Growth up to 10 and Scale up to 30. Kiro does not publish a dollar value for the credits, so this post lists the value as not published.

## Who qualifies

The offer is for early-stage to Series A startups with an official business email on the startup's own domain and an AWS account ID that uses a matching email. Applicants must be 18 or over. Startups with active AWS Activate credits are not eligible, although Activate credits can be spent on Kiro. Kiro's terms exclude individuals living in a list of countries, including Argentina, Brazil, China, France, Germany, Italy, Mexico, Poland, Russia, Spain and the UAE; check the terms for the full list.

Kiro's terms say the promotion runs from April 7, 2026 to December 31, 2026, allows one subscription per startup, and that credits expire one year after issuance. Once credits are used up, the account switches to standard Kiro Pro+ billing. Exceeding your tier's user limit, or choosing a plan above Pro+, may incur fees. AWS can modify or cancel the offer at any time.

Check kiro.dev/startups and kiro.dev/startups/terms for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Sign in with your AWS account from kiro.dev/startups and choose Apply for startup credits.
2. Pick the Starter, Growth or Scale tier and submit the form with your startup details.
3. Wait for the email decision. If approved, credits are added to your AWS account automatically.
4. To use them, log in to Kiro through AWS IAM Identity Center; credits do not work with Builder ID or social logins.
