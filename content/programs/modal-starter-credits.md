## What it is

Modal's Starter plan costs $0 plus compute, and includes $30 of compute credits each month. This is an ongoing monthly allowance, not a one-time trial.

## What you can use it for

GPU, CPU and memory time on Functions and Sandboxes, billed by the second, plus up to 1 TiB of volume storage and 1 TiB of network egress per month. The included compute does not cover Shared Endpoints, which are billed per token from the first request.

## Who qualifies

Starter is for small teams and independent developers. It includes three workspace seats, 100 containers and 10 GPU concurrency. You are billed when you go over the $30 of included compute, or when you use something the allowance does not cover. The Team plan, at $250 plus compute, includes $100 of credits per month, but that is bundled with the Team fee.

Check modal.com/pricing for the current allowance.

## How to apply

1. Sign up for Modal and stay on the Starter plan.
2. Run workloads against the $30 monthly compute allowance.
3. Set a workspace budget if you want to cap spend once the included credits are used.
