## What it is

Databricks Free Edition is a no-cost version of Databricks for students, educators, hobbyists and anyone learning or experimenting with data and AI. Databricks says it replaced the legacy Community Edition, which was retired in 2025. It is not a startup credit program.

## What you can use it for

Querying and visualizing real data in notebooks or the SQL editor, building and prototyping AI applications and agents, training ML models, ingesting data with Lakeflow, and using Genie for natural-language questions about your data.

## Who qualifies

Free Edition is for personal use and available at no cost to qualified users. It is serverless-only and quota-limited under Databricks' fair usage policy. Limits listed in the docs include one SQL warehouse, a maximum of 5 concurrent job tasks per account, one active pipeline per pipeline type, up to 3 Databricks Apps per account (each stops 24 hours after it is started or redeployed), one Lakebase project per account with scale-to-zero compute, and no custom models on GPU or batch inference. If you exceed your quota, compute is shut down for the rest of the day, and in extreme cases the rest of the month; your data and settings are kept.

There is no guaranteed reliability, support or SLA. Some limits can be raised by verifying your identity with LinkedIn. To use the full platform, you need a separate Databricks account.

## How to apply

1. Go to the Databricks Free Edition signup page and choose a signup method.
2. Databricks creates a new workspace for you.
3. Follow the tutorials to query data or prototype agents, staying within the quotas.
