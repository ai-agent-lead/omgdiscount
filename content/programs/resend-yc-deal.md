## What it is

Resend is an email API for developers. Its YC Deal page offers Y Combinator companies credits and a direct line to the Resend team.

## What you can use it for

Sending transactional and marketing email from your product. The deal's credits count toward Resend usage.

## Who qualifies

Resend's page offers $25,000 in credits for the current batch for 12 months, or $15,000 for YC alumni. It says to sign up before Demo Day for the current-batch gift. Other perks listed are a dedicated Slack channel with the founders and engineers, an optional feature on the Resend website, and direct access to the CEO by WhatsApp or text.

Companies outside YC are not covered. Resend's pricing page shows a free-forever plan with 3,000 emails a month and a 100 emails per day limit, and says there is no free trial.

Check resend.com/yc for the current terms.

## How to apply

1. Open resend.com/yc and sign up as a YC company.
2. Sign up before Demo Day if you are in the current batch.
3. Use the credits on your Resend account.
