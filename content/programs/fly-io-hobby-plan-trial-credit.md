## What it is

Fly.io used to sell Hobby, Launch and Scale plans. Official docs say those plans stopped being sold on 7 October 2024. Customers who signed up for the deprecated $5 per month Hobby plan, before Pay As You Go, received a one-time $5 free trial credit. This is not the current $1, 7-day trial for new accounts.

## What you can use it for

The $5 credit was a one-time trial on the personal organization created at signup, with no free allowances during the trial. When the credit was used up, Fly.io placed the organization on the $5 per month Hobby plan, which included $5 per month of usage and the plan's free allowances. Official docs say that legacy trial credit does not expire for organizations that already received it.

## Who qualifies

New accounts cannot get this credit or join the Hobby plan. Fly.io still honors legacy allowances for organizations that purchased Hobby, Launch or Scale before 7 October 2024, unless they convert to Pay As You Go, delete their payment method, or stop using Fly.io. Switching to Pay As You Go cannot be reversed back to Hobby.

New users should use the current $1 free trial and, after adding a card, the waiver for monthly bills under $5. Check docs.fly.io/about/discontinued-plans for the current legacy terms.

## How to apply

1. Do not sign up expecting this $5 Hobby trial. It is not offered to new customers.
2. If you already received the credit on a legacy Hobby organization, it still applies only to that personal organization, per the discontinued-plans docs.
3. For a new account, follow the current free trial and free waiver docs instead.
