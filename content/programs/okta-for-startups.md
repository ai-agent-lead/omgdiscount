## What it is

Okta for Startups gives early companies free identity tools for employees, contractors and partners through the Okta Identity Cloud.

## What you can use it for

Managing workforce access for up to 25 users with Okta IT Products, which include single sign-on, automated provisioning and deprovisioning, and basic MFA. Standard support is included. This covers workforce identity; for customer-facing login see the separate Auth0 for Startups post.

## Who qualifies

Okta's page does not list eligibility rules and tells applicants to reach its team through the form on the page, or by email to oktaforstartups@okta.com, for details.

## How to apply

1. Open the Okta for Startups page.
2. Submit the contact form, or email oktaforstartups@okta.com.
3. Okta's team confirms eligibility and sets you up.
